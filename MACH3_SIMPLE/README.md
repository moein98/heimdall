# MACH3-SIMPLE (revision A1)

A Mach3 parallel-port breakout board: six buffered step/dir axes, six 24 V
sensor inputs, a handwheel switched between jog and THC, six relays driven
over RS232 by an ATmega328P, and an isolated 0-10 V spindle-speed output with
FWD / REV for a VFD. It reproduces a board already in service and matches its
connector positions. It takes one supply, 24 V; the 5 V rail is made on the
board. Apart from the VFD port there is no galvanic isolation.

|---|---|

## What changed from A0

| | A0 | A1 |
|---|---|---|
| Supplies | 5 V and 24 V, each on its own terminal | **24 V only.** An LM2596S-5.0 buck (`U4`) makes +5V, in the top-right corner beside the 24 V input |
| Relay drivers | 2N7002 drain and source swapped - every relay **stuck on** | Fixed. `check_design.py` now checks every MOSFET's source is on ground |
| Top-edge terminal legends | Printed in reverse: `+24` over 0V, `+5` over the handwheel's B, `EMG` over X home | Fixed: the signals follow the legends, which follow the board in service |
| Input optocouplers | PC847 on a SOIC-16 footprint, which no PC847 fits | SMD DIP-16 (2.54 mm), the package PC847 comes in |
| Opto LED protection | Promised in the notes, missing from the circuit | A reverse diode across every input LED (`D20`-`D25`) |
| Spindle | - | **New:** isolated 0-10 V (`AVI`/`ACM`) and FWD/REV (`FWD`/`REV`/`DCM`) on `J5`, from port 2 pins 7, 8, 9 |

## Regenerating

The schematic and board are generated by scripts, not drawn by hand. To change
the design, edit `tools/build_project.py` or `tools/build_pcb.py` and
regenerate; an edit made in KiCad is lost at the next regeneration.

    python tools/build_project.py                  # schematic
    D:/KiCad/bin/python.exe tools/build_pcb.py --force   # placement (discards routing)
    D:/KiCad/bin/python.exe tools/autoroute.py export    # then Freerouting, then:
    D:/KiCad/bin/python.exe tools/autoroute.py import
    D:/KiCad/bin/python.exe tools/autoroute.py stitch
    KICAD_CLI=D:/KiCad/bin/kicad-cli.exe sh tools/validate.sh
    KICAD_CLI=D:/KiCad/bin/kicad-cli.exe sh tools/fab.sh

`build_pcb.py` needs the netlist that `validate.sh` exports (`review/net.net`),
so after a schematic change run `validate.sh` once before placing. It prints
which signal lands under each word of every top-edge legend - read those lines.

## Firmware

`sh firmware/build.sh` runs the tests and builds the hex;
`sh firmware/build.sh flash` also sets the fuses and programs the chip through
the ICSP header `J60` with a USBasp. Serial is 9600 8N1. Commands, sent with
Mach3's `SendSerial`: `A`-`F` relay on, `a`-`f` off, `T` handwheel to THC,
`t` handwheel to jog, `?` status. A1 needs no firmware change.

## Spindle, in Mach3

Port 2 pin 7 is the spindle PWM pin (PWMBase Freq 100 Hz), pin 8 is Output #1
(M3, FWD) and pin 9 Output #2 (M4, REV), all active high. Trim `RV1` for
10.0 V at full PWM. `docs/COMMISSIONING.md`, steps 6 to 8, has the settings.

`mach3 rs232 inputs 6 outputs 6 - pulse 5v - pnp sens.pdf` is the assembly
drawing of the board in service that the layout was copied from.
